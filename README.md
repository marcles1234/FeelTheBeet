This is a bee themed rhythm game! Whilst you are sat in a honeycomb cell, worker bees approach you (via the six corners). You are tasked as a queen bee to collect the royal jelly that your worker bees are providing you via the ‘w’, ‘e’, ‘a’, ‘d’, ‘z’ and ‘x’ keys (they make a hexagon shape on the keyboard). Try and collect the jelly too early and the bee won’t be in reach – too late and the worker bee has gotten bored and moved on.
